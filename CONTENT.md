RUT (Rengöring, Underhåll, och Tvätt) as of 2026
1. The Deduction Rate50% of the labor cost: Families only pay half of the total hourly labor charge.  
2. Excluded Costs: The deduction does not apply to materials, travel expenses, etc
3. The Annual Cap (Takbelopp)75,000 SEK per person, per year: Each adult in a household has their own individual cap.  Combined Households: If a family has two adults (e.g., a couple), they can collectively deduct up to 150,000 SEK per year.  

To qualify for the RUT deduction, the service  provided must meet these criteria:
Child's Age: The child must be in Grade 7 or lower (usually up to age 13).  
Location: The care must be provided in or in close proximity to the child's home (this includes picking up/dropping off at school or activities).  
Payment: The parent must pay you electronically (invoice/bank transfer) so there is a digital trail for Skatteverket.
