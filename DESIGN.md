# DESIGN.md - Mari's Visual Identity

## Color Strategy: Committed
A palette that feels "warm" and "rich", using soft earthy tones with a sophisticated touch.

### Palette (OKLCH)
- **Neutral Background**: `oklch(0.98 0.01 70)` - A very light, warm "paper" or "silk" tint.
- **Text Primary**: `oklch(0.25 0.02 70)` - A deep, warm charcoal/brown for readability.
- **Accent Primary**: `oklch(0.45 0.08 30)` - A rich "Dusty Rose" or "Terracotta" (warm, maternal).
- **Secondary Tint**: `oklch(0.92 0.03 70)` - A soft "Cashmere" for cards or sections.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Serif - **Baskervville** (Elegance, traditional trust). Weight: 400 (it's a high-contrast serif, weight comes from its shape).
- **Body**: Sans-Serif - **Hanken Grotesk** (Clean, warm-humanist, modern). Weight: 400.
- **Line Length**: 65ch for readability.

## Elevation & Spacing
- **Spacing**: Generous, rhythmic. 4rem+ between major sections to feel "unhurried".
- **Elevation**: Minimal. Use background tints and subtle borders instead of heavy shadows.
- **Borders**: `1px solid oklch(0.25 0.02 70 / 0.1)`.

## Motion
- **Entry**: Gentle fade-ups with `ease-out-expo`.
- **Interactions**: Subtle scale or opacity shifts. Nothing jarring.

## Components
- **The "Mother's Note"**: A distinct callout style with a slightly different background tint and elegant typography for personal anecdotes/philosophy.
- **The "Certification Seal"**: A clean, elegant badge style for CPR, bilingualism, etc.
