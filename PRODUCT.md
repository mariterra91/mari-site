# PRODUCT.md - Mari's Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Users
- **Primary**: Wealthy families seeking premium, reliable, and nurturing childcare.
- **Secondary**: High-end domestic staffing agencies looking for top-tier candidates.

## Product Purpose
A digital portfolio and CV that showcases Mari's unique blend of professional nanny expertise and motherly warmth. It must command the premium rate she deserves while feeling deeply personal and trustworthy.

## Brand
- **Core Identity**: The Gold Standard of Nurturing Care.
- **Promise**: Peace of mind through professional expertise and maternal intuition.
- **Visual Metaphor**: A sunlit, high-end nursery; soft cashmere; handcrafted wooden toys.

## Tone
- **Warm & Nurturing**: Feels like a safe embrace.
- **Professional & Competent**: No doubt about her skills and experience.
- **Intimate & Elegant**: Private, exclusive, yet welcoming.
- **Rich & Sophisticated**: Reflects the lifestyle of her target clients.

## Anti-References
- **"Daycare" Playful**: No primary colors, comic sans, or childish clip-art.
- **"Corporate" Cold**: No generic stock photos of people in suits or sterile office vibes.
- **"SaaS" Minimal**: Avoid the "blank white screen with blue buttons" look.
- **"Overly Busy"**: Must feel calm and serene, not chaotic.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Intimate Elegance**: Every design choice must feel both premium and personal.
2. **Trust through Experience**: Highlighting her dual identity as a professional and a mother.
3. **Serene Rhythm**: The layout should have a calm, rhythmic flow, mirroring a well-balanced day with a child.
